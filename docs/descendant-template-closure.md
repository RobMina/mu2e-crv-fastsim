# Tier-1b position-conditioned templates: never-visited layers and near-miss muons

## Decision

Keep both components **opt-in** (`tier1b-apply ... --topology-sidecar SIDE.npz --template-sidecar T.npz
--unvisited-layers --near-miss`; arm options `unvisited`, `nearmiss`), and use them together on top of the topology
placement. On the 10M held-out events (shards 21-220) they generate the two muon-descendant populations that
placement could not reach: never-visited-layer energy goes from 0.12-0.51 of G4 to 0.74-0.91, and the descendants
of muons with no crossing from a structural zero to 0.49-0.97. Descendant yields close in every sector within 1.7
clustered SE (M -16.8 % -> -3.2 %, T1 -3.4 % -> -1.2 %). Step bar falls 41.5 -> 20.3 (deck), posx 65.6 -> 47.8,
EX/y 6.80 -> 2.91, EX/z 6.95 -> 4.10, M/y 4.60 -> 1.46, and every cluster y score at cluster level improves.
**The step-level closure target is still not met**: the remaining residual is in the same-bar descendant steps
(their number per crossing and position across the bar), which these components do not touch (below). Defaults,
CCP and topology stay bit-identical.

## The two populations

The extracted stack is, top to bottom, M (y 4640-4719 mm, bars along z), T2 (4527-4635, along x), EX (4377-4485,
along x) and T1 (4227-4335, along z); T1's footprint lies inside EX's. After topology placement
(`descendant-placement-closure.md`) the step residual is in two muon-descendant populations:

1. **Rows in a layer plane the library muon never visits** (117,637 library rows, 33.5 GeV of 270 GeV other-bar).
   They come mostly from crossings of EX: into EX lower layers (8.0 GeV), T2 (6.3), T1 (5.3) and M (3.9). Their
   rate depends on whether the muon stops after the crossing: the last crossing of a muon with |p| < 150 MeV/c
   carries ~1.8 MeV of such energy per crossing (98 % later than 50 ns: decay electrons), any other crossing
   0.005-0.05 MeV (prompt deltas into the sector beside or below). The CCP placement cannot keep them where they
   belong: their offset points to a sector that is often absent at the fast crossing (T1 covers a third of EX), so
   `point` finds no bar and `nearest_bar` moves them into the crossing's own sector.
2. **Descendants of muons with no CRV crossing in their event** (66,141 donor steps from 2,044 muons, never
   generated by the sampler). Only 4 steps in two donors come from events where another muon crosses, so the event
   definition the library build uses (`desc_steps_unattached`) is the per-muon one.

## Design

**Unvisited-layer component.** The library is unchanged; a template sidecar marks the unvisited rows (other-bar rows
whose bar lies in a layer plane that the crossing's own muon never visits, from the donors' muon visits) and stores
per library crossing its entry, |p| and whether it is the last crossing of its muon. With `unvisited`:

- the main draw (any placement) leaves these rows out; it consumes no different random numbers;
- after the showers, each fast crossing draws one library crossing from its **position cell**: layer plane,
  width rank // 8 and along-bar entry // 400 mm, the set of library groups its muon crosses (a T2 deposit is an
  unvisited one only for a muon that misses T2), last-crossing flag and |p| class (150, 300, 1500 MeV/c). A cell
  with fewer than 20 library crossings falls back to (width // 16, along // 800 mm), then the layer plane, then the
  layer plane and the library group without the group set, always with the flag and |p| class;
- that crossing's unvisited rows are moved by the entry difference (both are in the same cell, at most a cell size
  apart) when the moved step lies in a bar of the same layer plane that the receiving muon does not visit; otherwise
  they keep their library position and bar. Times follow the fast crossing; no step is dropped, no energy scaled.

The position cell is the edge conditioning: it fixes the crossing's distance to every neighbouring sector edge to
within a cell, so a crossing beside T1 draws T1 deposits and one above T1's gap does not.

**Near-miss component.** One template per donor muon with descendant steps and no crossing in its event, at
absolute positions with times relative to the muon's generation time, keyed by cells of the generated muon:
the library group whose axis-aligned box its straight generated ray comes closest to (`closest_approach`; ties at
distance 0 go to the box entered first, i.e. the top of the stack), the distance class (enters the box, < 100, 300,
1000, 3000 mm, beyond) and, for EX only, the kinetic-energy class (300, 1000, 3000, 10000 MeV); the other groups
hold too few near misses to split. The reach probability is templates over the generated muons of the same cell
in events without a G4 primary-muon crossing, from the generator census of `g4ref_v1` shards 1-10 (the shower
denominator's census: 500k events, 350,179 such muons) scaled to the library's 10M generated events. With
`nearmiss`, after the showers (and the unvisited draw), every generated muon of a fast event with no fast crossing
reaches the CRV with its cell's probability and takes a uniform template of the cell; its particles get the muon
as parent. The sidecar recovers exactly the library's 66,141 unattached steps.

## Development (shards 11-20, 500k events)

The candidate was fixed on the development shards before any evaluation shard was read. Exact clustered chi2/ndf
vs G4, topology -> templates (both components): bar 2.21 -> 1.82, all/y 2.40 -> 1.96, T1/y 2.84 -> 2.18, M/y 2.92
-> 1.80, EX/z 1.11 -> 0.97; T2/y 3.01 -> 2.95. Muon descendants only: bar 1.15 -> 0.53, T1/y 3.13 -> 2.11, M/y 3.48
-> 1.69. Descendant yields: M -11.9 -> +3.3 %, T1 -5.8 -> -3.3 %. Two variants were tried and set aside:

- position cells without the crossed-group set: within development noise of the chosen cells (T2 unvisited energy
  0.54 vs 0.62 of G4); the group set was kept because it is the physical condition for a deposit to be unvisited;
- drawing the main library crossing within (bin, context, crossed groups): the descendant-only scores improve
  (T1/y 1.08, M/y 0.64) but all-step EX/y goes 0.82 -> 1.36 and lE 5.2 -> 7.0, because it changes the per-crossing
  descendant mix; and it does not remove the EX far-side excess (below). Not kept.

## EX far-side excess

Traced on the development shards with the topology arm: of the EX descendant rows placed more than 3 bars beyond
the receiving muon, 9,641 steps (1.59 GeV) are placed by their library topology, which is the G4 amount (~1.5 GeV).
The excess (~0.9 GeV, fast ~2.5 GeV) is the two fallbacks to the CCP placement, which put rows into the nearest bar
of the crossing's own sector, i.e. the EX top layer (T2/M targets above) and bottom layer (T1 below):

- never-visited-layer rows: 1,995 steps / 0.50 GeV; the unvisited component removes them (side>+3 energy 1.68 ->
  1.44 of G4, side<-3 1.62 -> 1.31 on the development shards);
- topology rows whose visit the receiving muon lacks (it does not cross the sector the library muon crossed):
  2,157 steps / 0.41 GeV. Not fixed; the visit-matched draw above was the cheap attempt.

## What changed (all opt-in; defaults, CCP and topology bit-identical)

- `crvfastsim.tier1b`: `uv_cells`, `last_crossing`, `visit_mask`, `group_mask`, `group_boxes`, `closest_approach`,
  `nearmiss_cell`, `muon_rays`, `attach_templates`, `_unvisited_for_crossings`, `_nearmiss_for_gen`, the `unvisited`
  and `nearmiss` options of `sample` and `drop_unvisited` of `_desc_for_crossings` (and the diagnostic trace keys
  `source_uv`, `topology_hit`, `source_tvis`). `tier1b-apply --template-sidecar T.npz --unvisited-layers --near-miss`
  turns them on.
- `valhist.descendant_templates`: the template sidecar (built from the library's own donors and a generator census;
  checks the library crossings) and `cells`, the reach table and cell occupancy.
- `valhist.placement_closure run --templates T.npz` with the arm options `unvisited`, `nearmiss`.

## Bit identity

With the template sidecar attached and the template options off, every other arm is unchanged: `placement_closure run
--retained` gives default, CCP and topology tables identical to the retained tables of `descendant-placement-closure.md`
on held-out shards 21-40 (all 20 shards, exact frame equality). Through `tier1b-apply` on shard 21, default and CCP
equal crv `cbea0ae`, topology equals that study's topology table, and `--template-sidecar --unvisited-layers
--near-miss` equals the closure run's template table (79,164 steps, 101,217 particles). The template draws come after
the showers', so a template arm differs from the topology arm only by the moved unvisited rows and the added near-miss
steps.

## Reproduction

Paths are examples. Code is this checkout plus a matching core on `PYTHONPATH`, all outputs new paths. The
transport, reference and topology sidecar are those of `descendant-placement-closure.md`.

```bash
# template sidecar from the library's own donors and the shower denominator's generator census (about 2 min)
python3 -m valhist.descendant_templates sidecar --manifest LIBRARY.json \
    --census-steps 'G4V1/crvsteps.{s}.root' --census-gen 'G4V1/gen.{s}.root' --census-shards 1-10 --out NEW_T.npz
python3 -m valhist.descendant_templates cells T.npz --library LIBRARY.root     # reach table, cell occupancy
# template arms in memory against G4 (20-shard chunks; join with the topology run of the same chunk in report)
python3 -m valhist.placement_closure run --library LIBRARY.root --sidecar SIDE.npz --templates T.npz \
    --transport 'TRANSPORT/fastC.{s}.root' --reference 'G4/crvsteps.{s}.root' --shards 21-40 \
    --arm tmpl=context,contain,point,topology,unvisited,nearmiss \
    --arm tmpl2=context,contain,point,topology,unvisited,nearmiss@2 \
    --arm tmpl3=context,contain,point,topology,unvisited,nearmiss@3 --arm uv=context,contain,point,topology,unvisited \
    --arm nm=context,contain,point,topology,nearmiss [--write tmpl=TABLES] --out RUN_21-40.json
python3 -m valhist.placement_closure report TOPO_21-40.json+RUN_21-40.json ... --layers topo,tmpl
# bit identity of the other arms with the sidecar attached: --retained ARM=PATTERN on a run without template options
# the same arm as a table
python3 -m crvfastsim tier1b-apply fastC.SUBRUN.root --library LIBRARY.root --outdir DIR --seed K \
    --crossing-context --contain-steps --point-placement --topology-sidecar SIDE.npz \
    --template-sidecar T.npz --unvisited-layers --near-miss
```

Measured on a 4-core GPVM: the sidecar 123 s (4.1 GB), five template arms 14-15 s/shard (6.4 GB per 20-shard
process), the NumPy cluster response 60 s/shard. The sidecar is 47 MB.

## Limitations

- The statistics are conditional on the fixed library, transport, reference and generator census. Donor and
  denominator uncertainty is not included; the diagonal scores are not calibrated p-values; floors from three seeds.
- The near-miss reach denominator comes from a 500k-event census. The cells where the generated ray enters the T2
  or M box without a G4 crossing hold 13 and 7 census muons but about a third of the template energy, so their
  reach probability has a 28-38 % Poisson error. A larger census needs only existing G4 tables of more training
  shards (no new production), at the cost of the development shards' independence.
- Near-miss templates sit at their library positions; they carry no correlation with the fast muon's position beyond
  its cell, and a geometry move does not move them (as for the showers).
- Unvisited-layer rows follow their library position within a cell (<= 400 mm and 8 bars), not the fast muon's path;
  their momentum direction is not rotated.
- The census labels a deposit relative to the union of its ancestor's crossings in a layer plane. It is a truth
  diagnostic, not a response model.
